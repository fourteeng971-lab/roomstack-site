Home inventory, room by room

Know what you own, before you have to prove it.

RoomStack photographs and catalogs everything in your home — room by room — so if fire, theft, or a flood ever hits, you have a complete, valued, photographic record ready to hand your insurer.

Free to start. No account, no sign-in, nothing collected. Pro is a one-time purchase — never a subscription.

Why RoomStack

Built for the afternoon you hope never comes.

Insurers ask for a room-by-room list with photos and values. Almost nobody has one, because making it is tedious. RoomStack’s entire design is about removing that tedium.

Photo-first, so it actually gets done

Snap a photo and the item already exists. Name, value, category, serial number — all optional, all addable later. A whole room takes minutes, not an evening.

Organized the way a home is

Property → Room → Item. Start from the ready-made “My Home” with common rooms already set up, then rename, add, or remove anything. Unlimited rooms and photos, free.

One honest number

Every item’s replacement value adds up into a single total, broken down by property and category. Items you haven’t priced are tallied separately, so the total never lies to you.

Reports an insurer will accept

Export a PDF with a summary page and one page per item — photo, value, details — or a CSV for spreadsheets and adjusters. The document you hope you never need.

Private by design

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, nothing collected. Your inventory lives on your device — and an optional Face ID lock keeps it to yourself. There is no server on our end to leak it.

Backed up on your terms

Free backup files you can save anywhere, plus optional iCloud sync into your own private iCloud account — encrypted, visible only to you, in sync across your devices.

Every detail

Serial numbers are what claims turn on.

An item can be just a photo and a name — or a full record with value, category, brand, model, serial number, purchase date, and as many photos as you want to take. Fill in as much as the item deserves and nothing more.

  • Unlimited photos per item, no cap on the free plan
  • Search by name, brand, or serial number
  • Deleted something by mistake? Undo brings it back, photos and all

Reports

The document you hand to the adjuster.

RoomStack Pro exports a PDF with a summary page — totals by category and by room — followed by one page per item with its photo, value, and details. Or a CSV, if someone wants it in a spreadsheet.

  • Whole inventory, one property, or a single room
  • Share straight to Files, Mail, or anywhere else
  • Free backup files too — save a copy outside the house

How it works

Three steps, one afternoon.

Walk a room

Open RoomStack, tap +, and the camera is already up. Photograph what’s in front of you and keep moving.

Fill in what matters

Add values to the things that would hurt to replace. Skip the rest — you can price anything later from its detail screen.

Keep the record safe

Export a backup or turn on iCloud sync, and generate a PDF report to store somewhere outside the house.

Questions

Good to know.

Is RoomStack free?

Yes. The free version covers up to 25 items across 2 properties, with unlimited rooms and photos, and free backup files. RoomStack Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the caps and adds iCloud sync plus PDF and CSV reports.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a single one-time purchase. It never renews and never expires, and it restores free on any device signed into the same Apple ID.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. RoomStack has no account system and no server. If you turn on iCloud sync with Pro, your inventory syncs through your own private iCloud account, which we can’t see.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Everything — capture, search, totals, exports — works with no connection at all.

What device do I need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. RoomStack is a universal app, so one purchase covers both.

What languages does it support?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Swedish, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese — following your device language automatically.
